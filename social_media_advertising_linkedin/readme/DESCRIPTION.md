This module plugs LinkedIn Ads into *Social Media Advertising*. Its lists
hang from a *LinkedIn* menu of their own, under *Advertising* and under
*Configuration* > Stages, so they never show the records of another social
media.

Main features:

- Campaign group and campaign management synchronized with LinkedIn Ads:
  create them in LinkedIn from Odoo (in draft state) or import the existing
  ones, and link posts to campaigns as sponsored creatives.
- The LinkedIn statuses of campaigns, campaign groups and ads (Draft,
  Active, Paused, Archived...) are declared as stages of *Social Media
  Advertising*, so the badges and the status bars show the real LinkedIn
  vocabulary.
